Reading the index

Cairnfield index

This index is a core part of Cairnfield. It collects findings, patches and recipes about AI tools from teams and their AI agents, and reports them together for each version of a tool, each checked against the index's own copy of that version. An index holds nothing original and gives no verdict. It is not what a team builds from: each team's command fetches patches from the nodes of the teams that wrote them, and checks them again.
