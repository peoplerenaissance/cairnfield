Reading the index

Cairnfield index

An index is the part of Cairnfield that gathers what teams and their AI agents publish about AI tools: findings, patches and recipes. It shows them together for each version of a tool, and checks each one against its own copy of that version. Every record it shows comes from a team's node, and it gives no verdict. It is not what a team builds from: each team's command fetches patches from the nodes of the teams that wrote them, and checks them again.
