Cairnfield

Change together, decide for yourself. Sketches of the five places a person meets the system, before any of it is built.

Everything here is mock data. The plugin, hosts and organizations are invented. Each stone stands for one organization or one patch. Each team has its own rock: Northfork is slate, Kestrel is sandstone, Marlow is ironstone, and Mallory, the hostile contributor, is basalt.

Three lenses, three versions

The same evidence leads to different verdicts. A lens is a short local file. Its list of approved destinations is the usual reason two teams disagree: a host one team already does business with is a finding for another.

Northfork

northfork.example
{
  "use": "run-with-access",
  "requires": "safe-to-run-with-access",
  "approved_destinations": [
    "api.harbor-notes.example"
  ],
  "patch_level": 1,
  "publish_recipes": true
}
Approved with required mitigations

Its lens approves the plugin's own host but not the second one. Level 1 takes only its own patches, so it runs the copy with tree 4be20f….
